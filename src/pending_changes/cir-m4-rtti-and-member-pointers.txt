CIR back end: run-time type information and pointers to members

The CIR back end configuration (BACK_END_TYPE_CIR) now compiles typeid and
dynamic_cast (pointers, references and void *; the static and dynamic forms
of typeid, with the null check of typeid(*p)), the typeinfo objects of every
type (fundamental, pointer, pointer to member, function, array and enum types
as well as classes, with their cv, incomplete and noexcept masks and the
linkage clang gives them, including the internal typeinfo of an incomplete
class and of a TU-local type), and pointers to data members and to member
functions: their types, constants (offsets, virtual functions, this
adjustments), .* and ->* (data and calls), conversions to and from base
classes, comparison and conversion to bool.  A type that contains a pointer
to a data member, whose null value is -1, is no longer written with zero
bytes: zero-initialization, value-initialization and new T() produce the null
value.  The other configurations are not affected.
